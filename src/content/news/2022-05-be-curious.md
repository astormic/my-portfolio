At the University of Leeds' annual public research festival, our stand pitted solar-powered racing cars against plants. Families raced small cars on a track, then compared how photovoltaic cells turn sunlight into motion with how chloroplasts turn it into chemical energy. Explaining the physics of light to a seven-year-old is one of the best tests of whether you understand it yourself.
