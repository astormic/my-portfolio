I spent a day as a science host aboard Moon Palace, a converted double-decker bus turned mobile observatory, with a rooftop satellite dish, a mounted telescope and screens showing Jupiter and Earth. I talked to children and parents about space, planets and the joy of seeing distant objects through optics, and could not resist sneaking in some microscopy too.
