The Your Entrepreneurs Scheme (YES) challenges early-career researchers to develop hypothetical but plausible start-up ideas. Our team, Caffinity Biotech, proposed using protein design and microbial fermentation to convert caffeine into a non-stimulatory molecule, for decaffeinated coffee that keeps its flavour. We pitched at the Food & Drink heat at Campden BRI and then at the national showcase at the Royal Society, London.

Team: Darcey Ridgway-Brown, Lawrence Collins, Amir Rahmani and Brian Mantilla, all from the Astbury Centre, University of Leeds.
