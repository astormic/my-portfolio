A collaboration between the Kaminski Group (Department of Chemical Engineering and Biotechnology) and the British Antarctic Survey, funded by UKRI, to build a high-resolution microscopy platform for watching protein dynamics in Antarctic fish cells at sub-zero temperatures. I use super-resolution methods and solvatochromic dyes, whose emission shifts with local membrane polarity, to sense changes in membrane organisation in real time without perturbing the cell.

- Developing a cold-compatible live-cell imaging platform
- Probing membrane dynamics at sub-zero temperatures with solvatochromic dyes
- Studying how Antarctic fish cell lines have adapted to the cold
