title: 'Dynamic live cell imaging at sub-zero temperatures'
date: 2025-03-01
kind: 'project'
period: 'Mar 2025 to now'
place: 'University of Cambridge and British Antarctic Survey'

Adapting live-cell and super-resolution microscopy to work at near-freezing and sub-zero temperatures, so that cells from cold-adapted organisms such as Antarctic fish can be imaged in the conditions they live in.
